Who we are

Zero to grid, one build season at a time

We're a first-year student team competing in SAE Supra, India's Formula Student-style competition — designing and building an open-wheel single-seater from scratch, then racing it against teams from across the country in 2027.

No car exists yet — which means every system, every layout decision, every line on the drawing board is still wide open. Whoever joins in the next few months isn't inheriting someone else's design. They're making it.
